What is renewable energy?

Renewable energy comes from sources that naturally replenish — sunlight, wind, and water — instead of being dug up and burned. Solar panels convert sunlight directly into electricity using semiconductor cells, with no combustion and no emissions at the point of use.

Once installed, a solar system keeps producing power for 25+ years with almost no running cost — the "fuel" is free. That's the whole economic case: you pay once for the equipment, then draw power from the sun instead of the grid for decades.

Why it matters

The sun sends more energy to your roof in an hour than your home uses all day.

The gap between what's available and what's actually captured is where solar and EV charging pay for themselves.
