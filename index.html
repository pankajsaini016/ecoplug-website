What is home EV charging?

A home EV charger is a wall-mounted or post-mounted unit wired directly to your home's power supply, letting you plug your car in overnight the same way you'd charge a phone. Most EVs can go from near-empty to full in 4–8 hours on a home charger, depending on the car and charger speed.

Because electricity is cheaper than petrol or diesel per kilometre, and you're charging during off-peak hours at home, drivers typically see a sharp drop in running cost — with none of the time lost queueing at a public charging point.

Why it matters

The best charging station is the one you never have to queue for.

A charger at home or at work turns "charging" from an errand into something that just happens while you're parked anyway.
