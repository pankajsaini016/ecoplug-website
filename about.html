Our story

We started EcoPlug because most homes are still paying for power they could be making themselves.

Pinachi Energies was founded in July 2026, and EcoPlug is our brand for everything solar and EV — from a single rooftop system to a charging point in your driveway.

Who we are

A local team, built for one job.

We're not a general electrical contractor that also does solar. Solar panel installation and EV charging infrastructure are the only two things we do, which means every visit, every quote, and every install is handled by people who specialize in exactly this.

We started EcoPlug with a simple observation: solar technology has gotten cheaper and more reliable every year, but most homeowners still don't know what a system would actually cost them, or save them. Our first job on every project is making that number clear before you commit to anything.

What we stand for

Three things we don't compromise on.

Straight numbers

Every quote shows expected output and payback time in plain terms, based on your actual roof and bills — not a generic brochure figure.

One team, start to finish

The people who design your system are the same people who install it and the same people you call afterward. No handoffs, no finger-pointing.

Built to outlast the loan

We size and install systems to run for 25+ years, because a system that underperforms in year three isn't a saving, it's a liability.

New to solar? Start with a free estimate.

We'll look at your roof and your last few bills, and tell you what's realistic — no obligation.
