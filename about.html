Our story

We started EcoPlug because charging an EV shouldn't mean planning your day around a queue.

Pinaki Energies was founded in July 2026, and EcoPlug is our brand for home and workplace EV charging — from a single driveway charger to multi-point setups for offices and apartments.

Who we are

A local team, built for one job.

We're not a general electrician who also installs chargers on the side. EV charging infrastructure is the only thing we do, which means every site visit, every quote, and every install is handled by people who specialize in exactly this.

We started EcoPlug with a simple observation: EV ownership is growing fast, but home charging is still confusing for most new owners — what charger to buy, whether their wiring can handle it, how long a real charge takes. Our first job on every project is making that clear before you commit to anything.

What we stand for

Three things we don't compromise on.

Straight numbers

Every quote shows expected charge time and running cost in plain terms, based on your actual vehicle and electrical setup — not a generic brochure figure.

One team, start to finish

The people who survey your site are the same people who install the charger and the same people you call afterward. No handoffs, no finger-pointing.

Safety first, always

Every install includes a proper electrical load check — a home charger pulls serious current, and we size the wiring and breaker to handle it safely for years.

New to EV charging? Start with a free estimate.

We'll look at your vehicle, your parking spot, and your wiring, and tell you what's realistic — no obligation.
